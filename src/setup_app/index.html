Job Hunter

Four steps, once. After that it applies every morning at 9:00 by itself.

1

Email and phone alerts

The daily summary and the 8:55 reminder are sent from your Gmail. Create an App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and paste it here. For phone alerts, install the free ntfy app and subscribe to the topic shown after saving.

2

Sign in to LinkedIn with Google

Opens a separate Chrome window just for Job Hunter. On LinkedIn's sign-in page click Continue with Google and pick your Google account — no LinkedIn password needed. Then leave that window open — the 9:00 run uses it.

3

Connect Google Sheets

Creates a sheet in your own Google Drive listing every job: applied ones ticked, the rest to apply on the company site. Questions the forms ask that need your answer appear in its Questions tab.

4

Start the daily 9:00 AM run

Your Mac will ask for your password once, so it can wake itself at 8:53. Keep the lid open and the charger in overnight.
